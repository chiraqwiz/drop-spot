Five stages. Rooftop, L Train, Lakefront, Club Sequin and South Side Street — pick one on the select screen or let it roll random. In a friend match, the host picks the stage.

FRIEND MATCH — PLAY ONLINE

Pick FRIEND MATCH • ONLINE on the select screen. One player creates a room and shares the 4-letter code; the other enters it. There is no lobby and no matching with strangers — the code only finds a friend who has the game open with a room waiting. Your browsers then fight directly, browser to browser.

In a friend match, each player uses the Player 1 controls on their own screen — keyboard, touch pad, or Xbox controller. Both players need to stay on the page for the whole match, and the fight can get laggy depending on both connections. If your friend's connection drops, the match ends.
